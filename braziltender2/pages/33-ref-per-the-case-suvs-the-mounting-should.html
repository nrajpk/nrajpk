a)ROUND: 450 millisecond pulse, 50 millisecond interval. Sequence: All modules on the left side of the bar should light up red for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the right side of the bar should light up in red for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the left side of the bar should light up in blue for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the right side of the bar should light up in blue for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, and the cycle should restart.COMPLYIn accordance with Ref. a), the vehicle will be equipped with the specified lighting system including LED signaling elements integrated into the vehicle bodywork for operational visibility and safety.Lighting System Layout (Image)
b)QTI: 250 millisecond pulse, 50 millisecond interval. Sequence: All modules on the left side of the bar should light up red for 250 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the right side of the bar should light up red for 250 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the left side of the bar should light up blue for 250 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, all modules on the right side of the bar should light up blue for 250 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 50 milliseconds, and the cycle should restart.COMPLYIn accordance with Ref. b), the vehicle will be equipped with the specified lighting system including LED signaling elements integrated into the vehicle bodywork for operational visibility and safety.Lighting System Layout (Image)
c)STOP: 450 millisecond pulse, 450 millisecond interval. Sequence: All modules on the bar should light up red for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 450 milliseconds, all modules on the bar should light up blue for 450 milliseconds, the bar should turn off completely for 450 milliseconds, and the cycle should restart.COMPLYIn accordance with Ref. c), the vehicle will be equipped with the specified lighting system including LED signaling elements integrated into the vehicle bodywork for operational visibility and safety.Lighting System Layout (Image)
d)EMERGENCY: Pulse of 100 to 150 milliseconds, 50 millisecond interval. Sequence: In this operating mode, the modules must be activated in a "disordered" manner in rapid pulses. At least 50% of the modules must be activated with each pulse, all in the same color spread across the bar, followed by an interval, with a new pulse in the other color (red pulse, interval, blue pulse, interval, front white pulse, interval, red pulse, interval, blue pulse, interval, front white pulse, interval...). It is the manufacturer's responsibility to balance the loads between the modules to avoid premature aging of any module relative to the others. The number of periods must be greater than eight and has no limit.COMPLYThe offered vehicle will comply with the requirement specified in Ref. d). The corresponding system or component will be implemented as part of the integrated armored vehicle configuration.Vehicle Technical Documentation (Image)
